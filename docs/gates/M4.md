# Gate report: M4 — Ewald summation for periodic electrostatics

Written by Claude on 2026-11-17. The team's two attempts at the first M4 module failed, and the
author then asked Claude to write M4 and M5 directly. The design and its equations are in
`docs/design/M4-architecture.md`. Suite on branch `claude/m4-m5`: 226 passed, 0 failed,
including doc-tests.

## Evidence per requirement

- **R4.1** (classic Ewald sum, exclusions, neutrality, erf precision):
  - The modules `ewald_real`, `ewald_reciprocal` and `ewald` (`ewald_self`, `ewald_correction`)
    implement the sum. Their four energies match an independent Python Ewald sum to 1e-9 kJ/mol:
    `parts_match_independent_ewald_sum`.
  - An excluded pair loses its nearest real-space image. The correction −k_e q_i q_j erf(αr)/r
    removes its reciprocal part.
  - A total charge beyond 1e-6 e is `NonNeutralSystem`: `net_charge_beyond_1e_6_is_an_error`.
  - `erf`/`erfc` are accurate to below 1e-13 relative (`tests/erf.rs`, 400-digit reference
    values). A dense scan of 20,000 points against glibc gave a worst relative error of 3.3e-15.
- **R4.2** (forces and virial of every part):
  - For the real-space part (with a short and a long cutoff), the reciprocal part, the
    correction and the total, the forces and the six lower virial components match finite
    differences from the R1.7 test kit to 1e-7 of the largest component. Every virial is
    symmetric. The self term has no forces and no virial.
  - The total forces and virial match the Python reference's finite differences.
- **R4.3** (validation):
  - Madelung constant of rock salt (8-ion cubic cell): 1.7475645946 to 1e-8 relative, at two
    splittings. Through the whole evaluator, the 64-ion fixture gives the same.
  - α independence: the total energy, forces and virial agree to 1e-9 relative for α from 3.5
    to 9.4 nm⁻¹.
  - Exclusion identity: excluding a pair changes the converged total by −k_e q_i q_j / r
    (minimum image) to 1e-10 relative, at two values of α. One test pair's unwrapped distance
    differs from its minimum image.
  - The finite-difference checks are listed under R4.2.

## Deviations and open issues

- **Superseded team plan:** the team's M4 plan (7 modules) was replaced. It repeated errors from
  the research answers that the machine check had flagged (the exclusion sign, a
  non-rock-salt Madelung cell and a wrong trace check), listed in the design document.
- **Cutoff:** the real-space cutoff may exceed half the cell height, because the image search is
  complete. The R3.5 restriction applies to the LJ cutoff only.
- **Performance:** classic Ewald scales as O(N^(3/2)) at best. No PME (out of scope:
  "performance tuning").
